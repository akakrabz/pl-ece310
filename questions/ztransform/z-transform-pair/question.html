{{#params.roc_choices}}{{text}}{{/params.roc_choices}} Split $x[n]$ into pieces that each look like one table pair. For a step that does not start where the exponent does, pay with a constant first: $a^{n}u[n-k] = a^{k}\cdot a^{n-k}u[n-k] \leftrightarrow \dfrac{a^{k}z^{-k}}{1-az^{-1}}$. The left-sided pair carries a minus sign: $-a^{n}u[-n-1] \leftrightarrow \dfrac{1}{1-az^{-1}}$, $\lvert z\rvert \lt \lvert a\rvert$. Right-sided pieces converge outside their pole, left-sided pieces inside, finite pieces everywhere except possibly $z = 0$ (samples at $n \gt 0$) or $z = \infty$ (samples at $n \lt 0$). A right-sided piece that starts at a negative index also excludes $z = \infty$; a left-sided piece that ends at a positive index excludes $z = 0$. Intersect the ROCs.
