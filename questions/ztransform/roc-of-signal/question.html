Consider the signal $$x[n] = {{params.x_tex}}.$$ What is the region of convergence (ROC) of its z-transform $X(z)$?

{{#params.roc_choices}}{{text}}{{/params.roc_choices}} Find the ROC of each piece separately; the ROC of the sum is their intersection. Right-sided exponential: outside the circle through its pole. Left-sided: inside it. Finite stretch: the whole plane except possibly $z = 0$ and $z = \infty$. Samples at $n \gt 0$ contribute negative powers $z^{-n}$, which blow up at $z = 0$; samples at $n \lt 0$ contribute positive powers, which blow up at $z = \infty$. That also applies to a right-sided piece that starts at a negative index (e.g. $u[n+2]$) and to a left-sided piece that ends at a positive index (e.g. $u[-n+1]$). If the pieces' ROCs do not overlap, the z-transform does not exist.
