(d) Two other sequences, $\{u[n]\}_{n=0}^{ {{params.Pm1}} }$ and $\{v[n]\}_{n=0}^{ {{params.Qm1}} }$, are to be linearly convolved with a radix-2 FFT: both are zero-padded to a common length $N$ that is a power of 2, and $u * v$ is computed as $\mathrm{FFT}^{-1}\{\mathrm{FFT}\{u\}\cdot\mathrm{FFT}\{v\}\}$. What is the minimum number of zeros that must be appended to each sequence?

The linear convolution of lengths $L_1$ and $L_2$ has $L_1 + L_2 - 1$ samples. A product of $N$-point DFTs is an $N$-point circular convolution, $\sum_{m=0}^{N-1} x[m]\,h[\langle n - m\rangle_N]$, which equals the linear result with every sample beyond $n = N - 1$ wrapped around: $y_N[n] = \sum_r y[n + rN]$, $0 \le n \le N-1$. The sample $y[N + i]$ is added to $y[i]$: the overflow lands at the start of the block, not at its end, and it is added, not dropped. Nothing wraps once $N \ge L_1 + L_2 - 1$. A radix-2 FFT also needs $N$ to be a power of 2, and both sequences are padded to the same $N$.
