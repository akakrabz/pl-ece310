A finite record $\{x[n]\}_{n=0}^{N-1}$ is an infinitely long signal multiplied by a window $w[n]$, so its DTFT is $\frac{1}{2\pi}X_d(\omega) * W_d(\omega)$: every cosine $A\cos(\omega_0 n)$ becomes two shifted copies $\frac{A}{2}W_d(\omega \mp \omega_0)$ of the window's spectrum. The rectangular window is $r[n] = 1$ and the Hamming window is $h[n] = 0.54 - 0.46\cos\left(\frac{2\pi n}{N-1}\right)$, both for $0 \le n \le N-1$ (and $0$ otherwise).

(a) Levels in dB compare amplitudes. Give both answers to 3 significant digits.

(i) {{#params.a_lect}}The {{params.a_win}} window's tallest side lobe lies ${{params.aL}}$ dB below its main-lobe peak (a level of $-{{params.aL}}$ dB).{{/params.a_lect}} {{#params.a_other}}A window's tallest side lobe lies ${{params.aL}}$ dB below its main-lobe peak (a level of $-{{params.aL}}$ dB).{{/params.a_other}} What fraction of the main-lobe peak height is that side lobe (an amplitude ratio)?

(ii) A weak tone has amplitude $A_w = \tfrac{1}{ {{params.am}} }A_s$, where $A_s$ is the amplitude of a strong tone. What is its level relative to the strong tone, $20\log_{10}\frac{A_w}{A_s}$, in dB?

(b) A signal with a strong and a weak tone is recorded for $N = {{params.bN}}$ samples: $$x[n] = {{params.b_sig}},\qquad 0 \le n \le {{params.bNm1}}.$$ The frequencies are far enough apart that the two main lobes do not merge with either window; only the heights matter here. With which window does the weaker tone's main lobe rise above the stronger tone's tallest side lobe? Select all that apply.

{{#params.d_choices}}{{text}}{{/params.d_choices}} An amplitude ratio $r$ is $20\log_{10} r$ dB: every factor of $10$ is $20$ dB and a factor of $2$ is about $6$ dB, so $-L$ dB is the ratio $10^{-L/20}$. A cosine's lobe has height proportional to its amplitude, so compare the weak tone's amplitude with the strong tone's as a fraction, just like a side lobe compared with its main lobe. Rectangular: main lobe $\frac{4\pi}{N}$ wide (edges $\pm\frac{2\pi}{N}$ from the peak), tallest side lobe $-13$ dB. Hamming: main lobe $\frac{8\pi}{N}$ wide, tallest side lobe $-42$ dB. Full-lobe separation of two tones needs $\lvert\omega_2 - \omega_1\rvert$ larger than the main-lobe width; in hertz, $\Delta\omega = \frac{2\pi\,\Delta f}{f_s}$.
