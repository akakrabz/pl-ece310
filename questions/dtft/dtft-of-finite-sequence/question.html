Consider the finite-length sequence $$x[n] = {{params.x_tex}},$$ where the arrow marks the sample at $n = 0$ (all samples not shown are zero).

(a) Compute the DTFT $X_d(\omega)$ from its definition. Type w for $\omega$ and j for the imaginary unit; exp(...), e^(...), cos and sin are all accepted, e.g. 1 + 2*exp(-j*w) or e^(-j*w)*(1 + 2*cos(w)).

(b) Evaluate the magnitude of the DTFT at $\omega = {{params.w0_tex}}$. Enter exact values: fractions such as 12/7 are accepted; decimals need 4 significant digits.

$X_d(\omega) = \sum_n x[n]\,e^{-j\omega n}$: each nonzero sample $x[n]$ contributes $x[n]\,e^{-j\omega n}$, with $n$ the true index (where the arrow is), not the position in the list. Mind the minus sign in the exponent. If the samples are symmetric (or antisymmetric) about some $n_c$, factor out $e^{-j\omega n_c}$ and pair the terms with $e^{jk\omega} + e^{-jk\omega} = 2\cos k\omega$ (or $e^{jk\omega} - e^{-jk\omega} = 2j\sin k\omega$). For the magnitude use $|e^{-j\theta}| = 1$, $e^{-j\pi n} = (-1)^n$ and $e^{-j\pi n/2} = (-j)^n$; a magnitude is never negative.
