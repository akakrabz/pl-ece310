Inverse DTFT: $x[n] = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} X_d(\omega)\,e^{j\omega n}\,d\omega$ for every $n$. Generalized Parseval: $\sum_n x[n]\,y^*[n] = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} X_d(\omega)\,Y_d^*(\omega)\,d\omega$. Line the samples up by their true index $n$, not by their position in the list. Parseval needs $Y_d^*$. Which sequence has DTFT $Y_d^*(\omega)$? Time reversal and conjugation: $y^*[-n] \leftrightarrow Y_d^*(\omega)$. So $Y_d(\omega)$ is the conjugate of the DTFT of $v[n] = y^*[-n]$.

Modelled on Quiz 2 (Oct 2026, “Parseval without squares”); inverse DTFT from Lecture 13, Parseval from Lecture 14.

(b) Inverse DTFT at $n = {{params.n0_tex}}$: $2\pi\,x[{{params.n0_tex}}] = 2\pi\cdot {{params.xn0_tex}} = {{params.B_tex}}\,\pi$.

(c) No conjugate: $Y_d = V_d^*$ with $v[n] = y^*[-n] = y[-n]$, so the value is $\sum_n x[n]\,y[-n] = (x * y)[0] = {{params.C_terms_tex}} = {{params.C_tex}}$ (not the answer of (a)).
