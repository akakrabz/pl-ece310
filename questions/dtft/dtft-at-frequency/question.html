({{params.mp_letter}}) At $\omega = {{params.wm_tex}}$: the magnitude, and the principal phase in $(-\pi, \pi]$ in radians (a decimal with 4 significant digits; an angle off by $2\pi$ is marked wrong).

$X_d(\omega_0) = \sum_n x[n]\,e^{-j\omega_0 n}$ with the true index $n$ (where the arrow is). $e^{-j\pi n} = (-1)^n$, $e^{-j\pi n/2} = (-j)^n$, $e^{j\pi n/2} = j^n$, and $e^{-j\pi n/3} = \cos\tfrac{\pi n}{3} - j\sin\tfrac{\pi n}{3}$ repeats every 6 samples. $\arctan(\mathrm{Im}/\mathrm{Re})$ only returns angles in $(-\tfrac{\pi}{2}, \tfrac{\pi}{2})$. If $\mathrm{Re} \lt 0$, add $\pi$ (when $\mathrm{Im} \gt 0$) or subtract $\pi$ (when $\mathrm{Im} \lt 0$). A negative real number has phase $\pi$.
