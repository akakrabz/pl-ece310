Two questions on the symmetry of the DTFT of a real-valued sequence.

(a) Each formula below gives the DTFT $X_d(\omega)$ of some sequence $x[n]$ for $-\pi \lt \omega \le \pi$ (extended $2\pi$-periodically). For which of them is $x[n]$ real-valued? Select all that apply.

(b) A real-valued sequence $x[n]$ has $X_d\!\left({{params.w1_tex}}\right) = {{params.X1_tex}}$. Find the values below. Enter exact values (integers here); the phase is the principal value in $(-\pi, \pi]$, in radians, as a decimal with 4 significant digits (an angle off by $2\pi$ is marked wrong).

$x[n]$ is real $\iff$ $X_d(-\omega) = X_d^*(\omega)$ for every $\omega$. Compute both sides: $X_d(-\omega)$ replaces $\omega$ by $-\omega$ (so $|\omega|$, $\cos$ and $\omega^2$ stay, $\sin$ and $\omega$ flip sign, $e^{-jk\omega} \to e^{jk\omega}$); $X_d^*(\omega)$ flips the sign of every $j$ and leaves $\omega$ alone. $X_d$ is $2\pi$-periodic: move the argument into $[-\pi, \pi]$ first. For the phase of $a + jb$ with $a \lt 0$, $\arctan(b/a)$ lands in the wrong half-plane: add $\pi$ if $b \gt 0$, subtract $\pi$ if $b \lt 0$.

Modelled on Quiz 2 (Oct 2026, “Hermitian symmetry as a test for a real $x[n]$”); Lecture 14 symmetry properties. Test: $x$ real $\iff X_d(-\omega) = X_d^*(\omega)$.
