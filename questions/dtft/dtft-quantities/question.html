$X_d(\omega) = \sum_n x[n]\,e^{-j\omega n}$. At $\omega = 0$ every exponential is $1$; at $\omega = \pi$, $e^{-j\pi n} = (-1)^n$. Use the true index $n$ (where the arrow is), not the position in the list. The inverse DTFT at $n = 0$ gives $x[0] = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} X_d(\omega)\,d\omega$, and Parseval's relation gives $\sum_n |x[n]|^2 = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} |X_d(\omega)|^2\,d\omega$. Do not forget the $2\pi$.
