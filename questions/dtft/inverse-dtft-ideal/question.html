$\dfrac{1}{2\pi}\displaystyle\int_{-W}^{W} e^{j\omega n}\,d\omega = \dfrac{\sin(Wn)}{\pi n}$: a rectangle of height $1$ and half-width $W$ is the sequence $\dfrac{\sin(Wn)}{\pi n}$, whose value at $n = 0$ is $\dfrac{W}{\pi}$. The full band ($W = \pi$) gives $\delta[n]$. Write $G(\omega)$ as a sum of centred rectangles (heights add; a band is a big rectangle minus a small one, a high-pass is the full band minus a low-pass). Then $e^{-j\omega n_0}$ is a delay by $n_0$: replace $n$ by $n - n_0$. At $n = n_0$ the value is the average height of $G$ over one period, $\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} G(\omega)\,d\omega$.
