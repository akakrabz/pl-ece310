{{#params.is_fir}} Convolution adds start indices and lengths: start of $y$ = start of $x$ + start of $h$, and $L_y = L_x + L_h - 1$. Run both rules backwards. The first sample of $y$ is (first sample of $x$)$\cdot$(first sample of $h$). Subtract that scaled, shifted copy of $x$ from $y$ and repeat with what is left; this is long division of polynomials in $z^{-1}$. Check: $x * h$ must give $y$. {{/params.is_fir}} {{#params.is_geo}} Find a combination of $x[n]$ and a delayed copy $x[n-1]$ that equals $\delta[n]$. By linearity and time invariance the same combination of $y[n]$ and $y[n-1]$ equals $h[n]$. Write $y[n]$ and the scaled copy of $y[n-1]$ under each other, aligned by $n$, and add column by column. $h$ is one sample longer than $y$. {{/params.is_geo}} {{#params.is_fir}}
