An infinite slab of charge occupies $-a < x < a$ (with $a = {{params.a_mm}}\ \mathrm{mm}$) and extends without limit in $y$ and $z$. Its volume charge density is $$\rho(x) = \begin{cases}\rho_0\,\left|\dfrac{x}{a}\right|^{ {{params.k}} }, & |x| < a\\[6pt] 0, & |x| > a\end{cases} \qquad \rho_0 = {{params.rho0_nC}}\ \mathrm{nC/m^3}.$$ Use $\epsilon_0 = 8.854\times10^{-12}\ \mathrm{F/m}$.

(b) Using Gauss's law, find the magnitude of $\mathbf{E}$ at the interior point $x = \tfrac{ {{params.xin_num}} }{ {{params.xin_den}} }a$, and at any exterior point $x > a$.

(c) Taking $V(0) = 0$, find the potential at $x = b = {{params.b_over_a}}a$.

$\rho$ is even in $x$, so $E_x$ is odd and $E_x(0)=0$. Use a pillbox with caps at $\pm x$: both caps carry flux, and $Q_{\text{enc}}$ is an integral because $\rho$ varies. $V(b) = -\int_0^b E_x\,dx$; split the integral at $x = a$ where the form of $E_x$ changes.

(a) $\rho_s^{\text{tot}} = \int_{-a}^{a}\rho\,dx = \dfrac{2\rho_0 a}{ {{params.k1}} } = {{params.sigma_nC}}\ \mathrm{nC/m^2}$.

(b) Pillbox with caps at $\pm x$: $2\epsilon_0 E_x A = A\int_{-x}^{x}\rho\,dx'$, so inside $E_x = \dfrac{\rho_0\,x^{ {{params.k1}} }}{ {{params.k1}} \,\epsilon_0\,a^{ {{params.k}} }}$, giving ${{params.E_in_str}}\ \mathrm{V/m}$ at the requested point; outside the pillbox encloses everything, $E_x = \dfrac{\rho_0 a}{ {{params.k1}} \,\epsilon_0} = {{params.E_out_str}}\ \mathrm{V/m}$ — the field of a sheet carrying $\rho_s^{\text{tot}}$.

(c) $V(a) = -\dfrac{\rho_0 a^2}{ {{params.k1}} \cdot {{params.k2}} \,\epsilon_0}$ and $V(b) - V(a) = -\dfrac{\rho_0 a (b-a)}{ {{params.k1}} \,\epsilon_0}$, so $V(b) = \dfrac{\rho_0 a^2}{ {{params.k2}} \,\epsilon_0} - \dfrac{\rho_0 a b}{ {{params.k1}} \,\epsilon_0} = {{params.V_b_str}}\ \mathrm{V}$ (negative: the potential falls moving away from positive charge).
