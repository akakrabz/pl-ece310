{{#params.regime_choices}}{{text}}{{/params.regime_choices}} $X_d(\omega) = \dfrac{1}{T}\sum_{k} X_a\!\left(\dfrac{\omega - 2\pi k}{T}\right)$: rescale the axis by $\omega = \Omega T$ (so the band edge $\Omega_B$ moves to $\Omega_B T$), multiply every height by $\tfrac{1}{T}$, put a copy at every multiple of $2\pi$, and add the copies wherever they overlap. The copy centred at $2\pi k$ is nonzero only for $\lvert\omega - 2\pi k\rvert \lt \omega_B$. At $\omega = \pi$ the copies at $0$ and $2\pi$ are equally far away, so either both or neither contribute. The copies are apart, touch, or overlap according to $\omega_B \lt \pi$, $\omega_B = \pi$ or $\omega_B \gt \pi$, i.e. $f_s \gt 2B$, $f_s = 2B$ or $f_s \lt 2B$.

{{#params.named}}These are the numbers of {{{params.source_html}}}. {{/params.named}}The band edge is $\Omega_B = {{params.WB_tex}}$ rad/s, i.e. $B = {{params.B_tex}}$ Hz, so the Nyquist rate is $2B = {{params.twoB_tex}}$ Hz. Here $f_s = \tfrac{1}{T} = {{params.fs_tex}}$ Hz, so $f_s {{params.cmp_tex}} 2B$: we sample {{{params.regime_html}}}.
