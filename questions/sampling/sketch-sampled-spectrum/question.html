The analog signal $x_a(t)$ has the real, even CTFT $X_a(\Omega)$ sketched below ($\Omega$ in rad/s): {{{params.words_html}}}, $$X_a(\Omega) = {{params.xa_tex}}$$

{{{params.fig_html}}}

It is sampled ideally with sampling period $T = {{params.T_tex}}$ s, $x[n] = x_a(nT)$. Sketch the DTFT $X_d(\omega)$ of $x[n]$ for $-\pi \le \omega \le \pi$ on the canvas below.

  • The horizontal axis (labelled $x$) is $\omega/\pi$: $x = 1$ is $\omega = \pi$, $x = 0.5$ is $\omega = \tfrac{\pi}{2}$, $x = -\tfrac{2}{3} \approx -0.667$ is $\omega = -\tfrac{2\pi}{3}$. The grid lines are $0.05$ apart.
  • The vertical axis (labelled $y$) is $X_d(\omega)$ itself, including the factor $\tfrac{1}{T}$.
  • Here $X_d(\omega)$ is piecewise linear, so it is fixed by its corners. With the Corner (point) tool, mark every corner of $X_d$ on $-1 \le x \le 1$: each point where the graph changes slope, and the two ends $x = -1$ and $x = 1$ (also where $X_d = 0$). Only the marked points are graded: every corner must be marked (within about 15 pixels), and any extra point must lie on $X_d$. Points can be dragged after placing them.
  • A Helper line (polyline, in the helper menu) is available to sketch the shape first; it is not graded (draw it as one line).
 {{#params.fn_ranges}} {{/params.fn_ranges}} {{#params.key_points}} {{/params.key_points}} {{#params.sol_pts}} {{/params.sol_pts}} $X_d(\omega) = \dfrac{1}{T}\sum_{k} X_a\!\left(\dfrac{\omega - 2\pi k}{T}\right)$: rescale the axis by $\omega = \Omega T$ (the band edge $\Omega_B$ moves to $\omega_B = \Omega_B T$), multiply every height by $\tfrac{1}{T}$, put a copy at every multiple of $2\pi$, and add the copies wherever they overlap. Only the period $[-\pi, \pi]$ is drawn. The corners of $X_d$ are where a copy starts, ends or bends: $\pm\omega_B$ (and the corners of a flat top) for the copy at $0$, and $\pm(2\pi - \omega_B)$ where the copies at $\pm 2\pi$ begin. If $\omega_B \gt \pi$ those copies reach into $[-\pi, \pi]$: add them to the central one there. Two ramps of opposite slope add to a straight (often flat) line.

{{#params.named}}These are the numbers of {{{params.source_html}}}. {{/params.named}}The band edge is $\Omega_B = {{params.WB_tex}}$ rad/s, i.e. $B = {{params.B_tex}}$ Hz and a Nyquist rate of $2B = {{params.twoB_tex}}$ Hz; $f_s = \tfrac{1}{T} = {{params.fs_tex}}$ Hz, so $f_s {{params.cmp_tex}} 2B$.

  • Axis: $\omega = \Omega T$ moves the band edge to $\omega_B = {{params.b_work_tex}}${{#params.trap_note}} and the corner of the flat top to ${{params.c_work_tex}}${{/params.trap_note}}.
  • Height: every copy is scaled by $\tfrac{1}{T}$, so its height is ${{params.H_work_tex}}$. The copy centred at $0$ is $\tfrac{1}{T}X_a\!\left(\tfrac{\omega}{T}\right) = {{params.central_tex}}$.
  • Copies: one centred at every $2\pi k$. We sample {{{params.regime_html}}}.
