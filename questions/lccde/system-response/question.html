(a) Find the output $y[n]$ for $n \ge 0$ (it is zero for $n \lt 0$). Enter it as an expression in n without $u[n]$, using fractions, e.g. 3*(1/2)^n - 2*(-1/3)^n.

(b) The first two output samples. Enter exact values: fractions such as -7/3 are accepted; decimals need 4 significant digits.

$Y(z) = H(z)X(z)$ with $X(z) = {{params.X_tex}}$, ${{params.Xroc_tex}}$. Factor the numerator and the denominator of $H(z)$, and before you expand, check whether a factor of the numerator of $H$ cancels a pole of $H$ or the pole of $X(z)$. After cancelling, $Y(z)$ is proper. Cover-up: $A_k = \big[Y(z)(1 - p_kz^{-1})\big]_{z = p_k}$; the causal output is $y[n] = \sum_k A_k\,p_k^n\,u[n]$. Check $y[0]$ and $y[1]$ against the recursion.
