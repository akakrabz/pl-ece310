 {{/params.has_loc}} Write each $H_k(\omega)$ as (a factor of modulus one) $\times$ (a real function $A(\omega)$). Factors such as $e^{-jM\omega}$, $j$ and $e^{\pm j|\omega|}$ have a continuous phase; a jump of $\pm\pi$ comes only from a change of sign of $A(\omega)$ ($-1 = e^{\pm j\pi}$). For an FIR, first pull out the midpoint of the taps. Range: $|a_1\cos\omega + a_2\cos 2\omega| \le |a_1| + |a_2|$, so if that is less than $|a_0|$, $A$ never reaches $0$. Two points of opposite sign ($\omega = 0, \tfrac{\pi}{2}, \pi$ are quick to evaluate) force a sign change in between. A zero is not enough: if $A$ only touches $0$ (write it with $c = \cos\omega$, $\cos 2\omega = 2c^2 - 1$, and factor), there is no jump.
