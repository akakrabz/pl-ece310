Time variation couples $\mathbf{E}$ and $\mathbf{B}$, and the coupled equations support waves travelling at $c$. This unit derives that, then studies the waves: their energy (Poynting), their description by phasors, what lossy and conducting media do to them, and what happens at an interface.
