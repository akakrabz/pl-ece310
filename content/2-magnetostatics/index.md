Steady current → static magnetic field. The structure mirrors Unit 1 with the roles swapped: $\nabla\cdot\mathbf{B}=0$ and $\nabla\times\mathbf{H}=\mathbf{J}$; Ampère's law plays the part Gauss's law played; the vector potential $\mathbf{A}$ plays the part of $V$; and the inductor is the circuit element that falls out.
